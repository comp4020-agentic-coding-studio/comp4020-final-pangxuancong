Chain Reaction

Build the level together. Survive what you built.

Rooms

Up to four players a room, each as one of four characters. Anyone else watches and takes a seat when one frees up. Each room keeps its own level.

    1. 1PickChoose a character, then stand under the map you want. Split votes are drawn by lot.
    2. 2BuildEveryone gets one piece, a bomb, or a skip.
    3. 3RunAll at once to the flag. +1 for making it, +0.2 when your trap gets someone.
    4. 4AgainThe level stays. First to 4 points, or six rounds.
