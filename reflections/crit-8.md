# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough did not happen at a keyboard. It happened while I was playing
*Ultimate Chicken Horse* online with my roommates. Before that, my plan for the
final project was a simple anonymous message board: technically on-brief
(multi-user, real-time, persistent) but with nothing that made it better because
other people were there. Watching the four of us place pieces into one level,
help each other, and wreck each other's runs showed me what co-presence feels
like when it is built into the object rather than added beside it. Chain
Reaction came from trying to keep that feeling while cutting the scope down to
something I could build well: fixed parts, tunable parameters, one shared
machine.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me realise that being a developer cannot only mean building. The
message board was the idea I would have produced if I had only thought about
what I could implement. The better idea came from paying attention to a product
someone else had already made, and asking why it worked. I want to look widely
at existing games, tools and apps and borrow from them deliberately, and to
treat that looking as part of the job rather than a distraction from it.
